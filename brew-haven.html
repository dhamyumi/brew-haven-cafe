Our Story

A haven brewed
with intention

Founded in 2018, Brew Haven was born from a simple belief: great coffee deserves an equally great space. We source single-origin beans from small farms in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Sumatra — roasted in small batches every week.

Direct-trade sourcing
We work directly with farmers, paying fair premiums and visiting origins annually.
In-house roastery
Our micro-roaster runs twice a week, ensuring peak freshness in every bag and cup.
Workspace-friendly
Fast fiber Wi-Fi, unlimited power outlets, and quiet hours every weekday morning.
